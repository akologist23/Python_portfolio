Japanese Preschool Teacher
Herndon, Virginia | 2026-2028 Academic Years

Overview
Soyo Kaze Youchien is seeking a Japanese-speaking teacher who will serve as the primary instructor for the preschool as we prepare to open on August 24, 2026.

Our program is designed for potty-trained children at least 2.5 years old. We hope to create a warm, small-group environment where children can naturally grow in Japanese language and culture through songs, stories, outdoor play, seasonal activities, and daily routines.

For more than 20 years, a Japanese preschool teacher in Herndon served local families, and with her retirement, we hope to continue that important tradition. Soyo Kaze Youchien is being created to carry that forward and provide a weekday Japanese preschool option in Northern Virginia.

This is not an assistant role. The selected teacher will be expected to lead the classroom, guide the daily flow of the program, and take primary responsibility for planning the educational content and curriculum.

Responsibilities
  • Collaborate with the Director on curriculum planning, program execution, and meeting Virginia licensing requirements.
  • Serve as the lead teacher for the Japanese preschool.
  • Develop and lead age-appropriate educational content and curriculum for children ages 2.5 to 5.
  • Plan and teach circle time, songs, read-alouds, crafts, games, and seasonal activities.
  • Incorporate kindergarten preparatory skills and activities into the program, including early literacy, listening skills, classroom participation, independence, and foundational math and STEM concepts.
  • Support outdoor play, meals, snacks, and daily routines.
  • Nurture children’s language, social, emotional, and cognitive development.
  • Take ownership of the daily classroom flow, structure, and learning environment.
  • Prepare teaching materials and maintain an organized, engaging classroom environment.
  • Responsible for daily cleanup, sanitizing, and classroom organization.

Onboarding Requirements
The selected teacher will need to complete standard onboarding steps required for a licensed home-based program, including background screening, fingerprinting, a simple state registration process, and CPR / First Aid certification. These are routine steps, and we will guide the selected candidate through the process.
Compensation
Compensation will depend on enrollment and experience. We already have interest in the program and expect to be close to full capacity. Compensation will start in August as a program initiation and planning stipend.
What matters most to us
At Soyo Kaze Youchien, education is at the heart of the program. We want this school to provide children with a strong foundation in Japanese language, early learning, and kindergarten readiness within a well-structured classroom environment. We are looking for a teacher who values educational purpose, and has the skills to create a high-quality preschool experience.
