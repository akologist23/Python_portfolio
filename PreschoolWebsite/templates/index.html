WELCOME!

Soyo Kaze Youchien is a private preschool preparing students for kindergarten while teaching Japanese language and culture.

WHAT WE OFFER

Japanese Language Immersion

Our instructors will communicate with students in Japanese and teach Japanese reading and writing.

Japanese Cultural Activities

Our activities focus on Japanese music, art, games, storytelling, and crafts, and include outdoor exploration and exercise.

Kindergarten Preparation

Our curriculum will develop social, emotional, and motor skills in students and will encourage problem-solving, critical thinking, and independence.

Who We Are

Soyo Kaze is a licensed family day home located in a quiet, residential neighborbood of Northern Virginia. It was started by the husband and wife team, Ricky Furusawa and Alicia Korol, to provide an enriching preschool education for their daughter that focused on teaching Japanese language and culture. The school employs one primary teacher and thus the number of spaces for children are limited. They expect the school to operate for the 2026-2027 and 2027-2028 academic years.
